DE3.52 / application counter 624 / LCD / 2026-11-01

Relative to DE3.51:
- Validate NFC SECTOR_SELECT framing before changing its pending state:
  packet 1 must be C2 FF, packet 2 exactly four bytes after HAL CRC removal.
  Malformed input receives NACK, exits pending selection and permits recovery.
  Valid sector mappings and permissive RFU handling remain unchanged.
- Correct the public information/QR destinations for MyMario Mario (Melody
  Car) and Toad. Their model IDs and tag contents are unchanged. Other model
  IDs retain the established NFC lookup URL. QR layout tests cover actual
  encoder output on the 128x64 LCD and decode the generated images.

This is an application-only update. BL8, S112 7.3.0, device pin mapping,
RAM/flash partitions, Packed LittleFS PFS1 and its exact storage engine are
retained. No formatting, migration or rewriting of the Amiibo collection.
All previous application fixes and features are retained.

Validation is bound to exact source and final ARM binary. Host regression,
native C, ASan/UBSan, ARM32 QEMU and signature checks are separate evidence.
Modeled peripherals do not prove physical NFC timing, wake behavior, radio
range or whole-device RAM/stack watermarks. Device installation remains a
separate step; known figure recognition should be checked after flashing.

NFC reference: NXP NT3H2111/NT3H2211 Rev.3.6, section10.12, Figure28.
